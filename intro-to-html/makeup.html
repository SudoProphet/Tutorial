Face Serum

Face serum should be applied after cleansing and toning, and before moisturizing, both morning and night. A pea-sized amount is usually sufficient. Apply it to damp skin, gently patting it in, and allow it to absorb fully before applying other products.

Morning: Serums with ingredients like vitamin C are great for morning use, as they help protect the skin from environmental damage and brighten the complexion.

Night: Serums with active ingredients like retinol or AHA/BHA are often recommended for nighttime use as they can increase sun sensitivity.
